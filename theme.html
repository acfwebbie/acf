Mission Statement

The Asian Christian Fellowship seeks to carry out the Great Commission of our Lord Jesus Christ to make disciples of all nations by proclaiming the Gospel, providing fellowship for edification, nurturing Christians in spiritual growth, and building up Christian leaders.

Our mission statement comes from Matthew 28:18-20, a passage known as the Great Commission. As Jesus' words after his resurrection, we believe that they should be as important to us as they were to Christ's apostles and disciples.

Theme for 2026-2027

The theme is a contextual way in which we can understand our mission statement as a fellowship. This year, our theme word is 'Renewed' and our theme verse is Romans 6:4. This points to the new life we have been given through Christ because we are united with Him in His death and resurrection. Because of His grace alone, we are no longer enslaved to our sin, but belong to Christ. Therefore, we ought to be continuously renewed by the gospel, faithfully love God, love one another, and grow in our relationship with Him.

“We were buried therefore with Him by baptism into death, in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, we too might walk in newness of life.”
- Romans 6:4 (ESV)
